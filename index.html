This is a fan site for Sneaky Sasquatch. I've been poking around inside the Mac version for a while, going through save files and scenes and building a mod loader for it, and figured I should put the useful bits somewhere other people can find them.

The game

If you haven't played it, it's an Apple Arcade game by RAC7 Games from 2019, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. You're a sasquatch living next to a campground, and the first hour is mostly you raiding picnic baskets while a park ranger tries to catch you.

Then it keeps going. Disguises, jobs, cars, boats, fishing, golf, racing, a town, a seaport, a story in chapters. It's a lot bigger than it looks at the start, which is part of why I got into taking it apart.
