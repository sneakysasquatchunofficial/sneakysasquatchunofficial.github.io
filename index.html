Hi. This is a small fan page about Sneaky Sasquatch, the Apple Arcade game where you're a sasquatch who lives in the woods and steals picnic food from campers. I play it way too much so I made a page for it.

What is the game?

It's made by RAC7 Games and came out on Apple Arcade in 2019. You can play it on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

At the start you just sneak around the campground grabbing food out of picnic baskets and coolers while the park ranger tries to catch you. Later it opens up a lot. You get disguises so people don't notice you, you can get jobs, drive stuff, go fishing, play golf, race, and go to new places like the town and the seaport. There's a story split into chapters too.
