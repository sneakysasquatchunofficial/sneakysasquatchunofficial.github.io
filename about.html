Sneaky Sasquatch (unofficial)

About

Currently, I create tools and mods for Sneaky Sasquatch as a hobby. Much of the content on this site originally was gathered as notes for myself as I attempted to understand how scenes and saves function within the game. At some point, I felt it was more appropriate to share this online rather than keeping it to myself.

My exploration of the game and examination of its files have all been performed on my own Mac. If you notice any issues or if the content has become outdated (due to updates breaking various aspects of the game), please share this through the FAQ page.

This site is ad-free, without tracking or accounts—only pages.
