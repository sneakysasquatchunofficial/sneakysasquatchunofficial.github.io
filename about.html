About

I make mods and tools for Sneaky Sasquatch as a hobby. Most of what's here started as notes for myself while I was figuring out how the saves and scenes work, and at some point it made more sense to just put it online.

Everything comes from playing the game and digging through its files on my own Mac. If something's wrong or out of date, and updates do break things, tell me through the FAQ page.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Just pages.

Fan made. Not the official site and not connected to RAC7 Games or Apple. Sneaky Sasquatch belongs to them.
