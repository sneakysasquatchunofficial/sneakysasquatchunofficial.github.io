Is this the official site?
No. Just a fan page. The game is made by RAC7 Games.

Where can I play it?
Apple Arcade, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Is it free?
It comes with an Apple Arcade subscription.

Is it on Android or PC?
Not that I know of. It's an Apple Arcade game.

Do you have mods?
Yes, for the Mac version, on the downloads page. They don't have any game files in them. Be careful with other sites that say they have free stuff or a modded game to download.

Do mods work on iPad?
No. iPad and iPhone don't let you get to the game's files.
