The usual ones

Is this the official site?
No, it's a fan site. RAC7 Games makes the game and has nothing to do with this.

Where do I get the game?
Apple Arcade. It's on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Does it cost anything?
It's included with Apple Arcade, so you need the subscription.

Android? Windows?
Not as far as I know. It's an Apple Arcade game.

Mods?
Yep, for Mac. See downloads. None of them include game files. If some other site is offering a pre-modded copy of the game, I wouldn't touch it.

Do the mods work on iPad?
No. iOS doesn't let you at the game's files, so there's nothing to edit.
