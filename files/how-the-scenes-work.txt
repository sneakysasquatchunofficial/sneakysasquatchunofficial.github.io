SNEAKY SASQUATCH - HOW THE SCENES WORK
=======================================
Notes from modding the game with SSMod (version 2.1.9, checked against 2.2).
Written in my own words from looking at the game's files and logs. No game code is copied here.


1. THE BASICS
-------------
- Sneaky Sasquatch is a Unity game. In Unity a "scene" is one saved world: every object in a place,
  where it is, and what parts it has.
- The game has 424 scenes.
- Each scene is stored as a file called levelN (level0, level1, ... level423) in:
      Sasquatch.app/Contents/Resources/Data/
- The numbers mean nothing by themselves. The file "globalgamemanagers" holds the build list that
  matches each number to a name. Examples:
      level7    Campground_CampsiteB
      level8    Campground_Hub
      level9    Campground_RVPark
      level118  Town
      level346  Seaport_Layer_Chapter 4 Rescue & Boss Showdown
- SSMod reads that list for you:  ./ssmod scenes [filter]


2. WHAT IS INSIDE A SCENE FILE
------------------------------
- A scene file is a list of objects, each with an id number (a "path id").
- The main kinds of object:
    GameObject      a thing in the world (a tree, a camper, a door zone). Just a name and a list of parts.
    Transform       where a GameObject is, its size and rotation, and which object it hangs under.
    Components      the parts that make it do things: sprites, colliders, physics bodies, sounds,
                    and the game's own scripts (MonoBehaviours).
- Objects point at each other by id. A camper's body points at its animation controller, its
  collider, its bones, and so on.
- A scene does NOT hold most art or sounds. Those live in shared files and the scene points at them:
    sharedassets0.assets     art and models used all over the game
    sharedassetsN.assets     art used by only one scene (N matches the level number)
    resources.assets         items, flags, sounds, weather, dialogue data and other game data
- So a scene is mostly "what is where and how it is wired up", not the pictures themselves.


3. KINDS OF SCENES
------------------
3.1 Areas (the places you walk around)
    Campground_Hub, Campground_CampsiteA/B/CD, Campground_RVPark, Campground_Lake, Campground_River,
    Campground_ParkingLot, Town, Stripmall, Seaport, Marina, Highway_West, Highway_4WayIntersection,
    Highway_East_MountainPass, Racetrack, DirtPark_Track, Golfcourse, GolfCourse_Long Drive,
    GolfCourse_DrivingRange, Farm, Farm Town, Island, Ocean, Ocean Rocky, Snow Main, Snow Peak,
    Snow Mid Mountain, Snow Hot Springs, Snow Track, SkiSlope, LumberMill, Museum, Bird Sanctuary,
    Apartments, Sewer and its rooms.

3.2 Interiors that are their own scene
    Campground_TreeInterior, Campground_CaveInterior, Campground_WaterfallInterior, Island_CaveInterior,
    Snow_CaveInterior, GolfCourse_CaveInterior, Farm_Cave, Office_Basement, Office_Floor_2 to _6,
    TownHall_Floor_2/3/B1/B2, PoliceStation_Floor_2, FireStation_Floor_2, Seaport_Office_Floor_2,
    Seaport_Office_Floor_Tower, Sewer_Fox_Den, Sewer_SecretWarehouse.
    Other buildings (shops, the bank, the RV) sit INSIDE their area scene instead. When you walk in,
    the game raises an "entered a building" event rather than loading a new scene.

3.3 Underwater scenes
    Many areas with water have a twin ending in _Underwater (Campground_Lake_Underwater,
    Island_Underwater, Marina_Underwater, Highway_West_Underwater...). Diving takes you there.

3.4 Results and event scenes
    GolfCourse_Results, Racetrack_Results, Snow EventResults: the podium and score screens after
    races and events.

3.5 Layers (the biggest group: 319 of the 424 scenes)
    A layer is a scene that is loaded ON TOP of an area, not instead of it. Its name is the area,
    then _Layer_, then what it adds:
        Seaport_Layer_Chapter 4 Boss Phase 4 (Satch Captured)
        Seaport_Layer_Chapter 4 Rescue & Boss Showdown
    Layers hold the extra characters, cutscenes, props and triggers for one story moment, one job,
    one event or one season. The area stays the same and the layers change what is in it.
    Areas with the most layers:
        Campground_Hub        50
        Town                  25
        Racetrack_Entrance    22
        Seaport               17
        DirtPark_Track        15
        Island                14
        Snow Snowball         13
        Golfcourse            11
        Farm                  11
        Snow Main             10
        Campground_ParkingLot  9
        Stripmall              8
    Example: the boss fight's Junior ("Camper (Pemby Jr)") and his "MonsterMuscles" live in the
    Seaport showdown layer, not in Seaport itself. That is why Monster Mode copies him from the layer.

3.6 Special scenes with no sasquatch to control
    Title                      the title screen
    Elevator_Transition,
    Elevator_Transition_Office the short elevator ride between floors
    ArcadeSimulatorGame        the arcade game world
    SurfGame                   the surfing minigame
    TestInputManager, Test_GameCenter   developer test scenes
    SSMod's "everywhere" skips all of these, plus every layer.


4. WHAT EVERY AREA HAS
---------------------
- LevelController: the scene's own manager object.
- AreaBounds: the edge of the walkable world.
- Custom Frame Bounds: how far the camera may scroll.
- Custom Pathfind Area: where NPCs are allowed to walk (they plan routes inside it).
- Exits: trigger zones near the edges. Each exit leads to another scene and a named entrance there
  (East, West, North, South, or a named spot). Walk into one and the game loads the next scene and
  puts you at the matching entrance.
- Doors: walk-through zones for interiors. Same idea as exits, but for buildings.
- Spawn and entrance points: where you appear when you arrive from each direction.
- Activators: objects that switch other objects on or off depending on story flags. This is how
  a place changes as the story moves on, without a new scene.
- Patrol routes: paths that campers and rangers walk along, with pauses and actions.
- Interaction spots: the things you can press (talk, sit, check, pick up), each with a trigger area.
- Sounds and lights: ambient sound zones, light sources for night.


5. HOW DEPTH AND DRAWING WORK
-----------------------------
- Physics is flat 2D: Rigidbody2D bodies and 2D colliders (boxes, circles, polygons).
- Characters are 3D models on standard humanoid skeletons, drawn into that 2D world.
- Things that are lower on the screen are drawn in front. Each object gets a depth (z) from its
  height on the screen (y), so the sasquatch walks behind a tree above it and in front of a tree
  below it.
- Some objects add a small nudge forward or back so things at the same height still sort right.
- Floors and ground pictures use a separate "background layer" system and stack on top of each
  other by layer number instead.
- Physics layers decide what bumps into what. The named ones include:
      0 Default      8 Player       9 Actor        10 Vehicle
      4 Water        12 Container   17 BuildingExterior
      23 WaterVehicle    26 DeepWater    27 VehicleBlocker
  Monster Mode looks for cars on layer 10 (mask 1024).


6. LOADING, LEAVING AND SAVING
------------------------------
- Going through an exit unloads the old scene and loads the new one. The game log shows it as
  "Unloading N Unused Serialized files" and "Unloading N unused Assets".
- Everything that belonged to the old scene is gone after that. This is why a mod that should
  work "everywhere" needs one copy in every area: about 98 scenes.
- A few managers survive scene changes and stay loaded the whole time (the backpack and inventory
  UI work like this). That is the idea behind the "faster install" plan: one manager that stays
  loaded instead of 98 copies.
- Layers are loaded together with their area when the story, job or event calls for them.
- The save file remembers which area you were in and which entrance you came through. The log
  shows it on load, for example:
      Resume game. level=Town entrance=East
- Saves also sync to iCloud. If your iCloud is full the log shows "Quota exceeded", but the local
  save still works.


7. CHARACTERS INSIDE A SCENE
----------------------------
- Every person and creature is a GameActor: it moves, animates, and raises events like entering a
  building, sitting in a chair or getting into a vehicle.
- Human NPCs are Campers. A camper has:
      a body (GameActor, physics body, collider)
      a look (skin, shirt, pants and shoe colours, outfit)
      senses: vision, hearing and smell, each its own child object
      an AI behaviour tree that decides what it does
      optional patrol routes and dialogue
- A camper's skeleton is a full humanoid rig (spine, arms, hands, fingers, head, eyes).
- In a scene, each character is one GameObject with all of those as child objects. Copying a
  character to another scene means copying that whole tree, plus anything outside it that it
  points to (like Junior's MonsterMuscles).


8. HOW SCENES TALK TO GAME DATA
-------------------------------
- Story flags (true/false switches) live in resources.assets. Scenes point at them for activators,
  conditions and dialogue.
- Items live in an item library in resources.assets. A pickup in a scene points at an item there.
- Dialogue and behaviour are NodeCanvas graphs: dialogue trees and behaviour trees stored as data.
  A scene object holds the graph and a "blackboard" of its variables.
- Weather presets (Clear, Rainy, Snowy, SnowyHeavy) are data assets too.
- Sounds are FMOD sound banks. Many have readable names like sfx_blastOff.


9. HOW SSMOD WORKS WITH SCENES
------------------------------
- SSMod never edits the game's code. It edits scene files and data files, then puts the edited
  copies into the game folder. A clean copy of every file it touches is kept in base/ so installs
  can be repeated and undone (./ssmod uninstall).
- Finding things:
      ./ssmod scenes [filter]            scene names
      ./ssmod where SCENE [filter]       named objects in a scene with their x, y
      ./ssmod assets CLASS [filter]      data assets, e.g. AudioAsset, WeatherData
      ./ssmod functions [filter]         game functions a script can call
- Adding things to a scene:
      npc, spot, pickup        built from copies of real game objects (a Lake Maze camper, Doctor
                               Duck's talk object, Town's "Check shoes" spot, a campsite pickup)
      place "Name" from "Scene" in "Scene" at (x, y)
                               copies any object from any scene into another, with options:
                               scale, size, color, rotate, flip, front, layer, text, bob, animate,
                               showIf / hideIf, as Name, with "Other", puppet
      hud                      text or pictures stuck to the screen
      area                     a brand-new scene, added to the end of the build list
      connect / door           new exits and doors between scenes
- "everywhere": SSMod makes one copy per playable scene (about 98), skipping layers, the title,
  test scenes, minigames and elevator rides.
- Copying between scenes: the copier brings along the object's whole tree plus the local assets it
  uses (meshes, materials). Pointers to other scenes' files are re-linked. Pointers to objects
  outside the copied tree are lost unless they are copied together with "with".
- Builds reuse unchanged scenes from last time, so editing one mod usually rebuilds only the scenes
  that mod touches.


10. HANDY FACTS
---------------
- Scene count: 424. Layers: 319. Playable "everywhere" scenes: about 98.
- Groups by name (all scenes, layers included):
      Campground 122, Racetrack 34, Town 26, Seaport 20, DirtPark 19, Island 17, Sewer 15,
      Highway 15, Snow Snowball 14, GolfCourse 14, Farm 13, Golfcourse 12, Snow Main 11,
      Stripmall 9, Marina 9, TownHall 7, Office 7, Museum 5, LumberMill 5
- The golf course appears under two spellings: GolfCourse_ and Golfcourse.
- The Campground hub is the busiest scene in the game: it has 50 layers of its own.
- Scene objects can share a name. In that case pick one by its path, like "Bank/Counter" or
  "Fire_Lit/flames".
- The RV park campfire's flames ("Fire_Lit/flames" in Campground_RVPark) are a plain particle
  effect, which makes them easy to copy. Monster Mode uses them for burning cars.
- Town has "Building Fire Elements" and "Door Smoke" objects for the firefighter job. They only
  show fire and smoke when a fire job is running.
