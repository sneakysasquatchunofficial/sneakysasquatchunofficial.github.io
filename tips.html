Tips

A few things for people just starting out. Nothing fancy.

Before going for a basket, stop and watch the ranger for a bit. He walks a route. Go when he's heading away from you, not when he's just turning around.

Bushes work better than you'd think. If he's getting close, hide and let him walk past.

If a camper is looking straight at the cooler, leave it. Coming back in a minute is always faster than getting chased.

Get a disguise as early as you can. Once people stop panicking when they see you, basically everything gets easier.

I spent way too long at the campground my first time. The quests are what open the rest of the map up, so follow them.

The side stuff like fishing, golf and racing is worth doing even when no quest is pushing you there.
