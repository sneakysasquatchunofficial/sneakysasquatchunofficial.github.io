Tips

Some tips for when you're new. Nothing crazy, just what helped me.

  1. Watch the ranger first. Before you go for a basket, stand still and watch where the park ranger walks for a bit. Go when they're walking away from you.
  2. Bushes are your friend. If the ranger is getting close, hide in a bush and wait.
  3. Don't be greedy. If someone is looking right at the cooler, come back later. The food isn't going anywhere.
  4. Get a disguise early. Walking around in a disguise makes everything way easier because people stop freaking out.
  5. Don't stay at the campground forever. The game gets a lot bigger once you start going to other places. Follow the quests and new stuff opens up.
  6. Try the side stuff. Fishing, golf and racing are fun even if you're not doing a quest.
