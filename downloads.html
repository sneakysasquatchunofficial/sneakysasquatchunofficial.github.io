Sneaky Sasquatch (unofficial)

Downloads

Mods and save stuff for the Mac version. None of this has game files in it, you need your own copy of the game. Back up your save before you mess with anything (how).

SSMod (the mod loader)

SSMod.zip (900 KB) - lets you add new stuff to the game: new places, people, items, keys, sounds. It changes a COPY of the game, the real one stays normal. Open START HERE.txt after you unzip it. Made by LPP Entertainment.

SSMOD.txt - the full mod writing manual
MOD-MAKER-GUIDE.txt - easier guide for new modders

Mods

SSMod-Big-Mod-Pack.zip (1.8 MB) - all the big ones: the Airport, the Sasquatch Zoo, the Bigfoot Diner with cooking, Pet Cat, Taxes, Ranger Pat, Trails, Weather Wizard, Duck Lab and more. Needs SSMod.

Small example mods (one file each, put in SSMod's mods folder):

  • sasquatch_powers.ssmod - keys 1 2 3, new powers
  • race_the_clock.ssmod - a coach gives you a countdown race
  • hotdog_stand.ssmod - hot dog guy that sells a new item
  • corner_shop.ssmod - a little shop building
  • custom_meshes.ssmod - your own shapes in the game
  • lpp_fan_club.ssmod - a sign with a secret password

Save editor

Save editor - change coins, speed limits, finish chapters, teleport. Works right in the browser.

Ready-made saves

MaxSave.zip - day 1386, 999999 coins, chapters 1 to 4 done, lots of stuff unlocked. Made on game version 2.2.1 (Mac). How to put it in. It replaces whatever is in that slot, so use an empty slot or back up.
