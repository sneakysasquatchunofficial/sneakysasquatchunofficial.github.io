Places

The main areas. I'll add more detail to these over time.

Campground

Where you start. Campsites, an RV park, a lake, a river and a parking lot. This is where you learn to sneak food and dodge the ranger.

Town

Bigger place with shops and people.

Golf Course

You can play golf and work as a caddy here. There's a driving range too.

Racetrack

Car racing. There's a dirt track nearby as well.

Seaport and Marina

The seaport is a big harbor that shows up later in the story. The marina is where the boats are.

Farm

A farm with a little farm town next to it.

Island

Out across the ocean. No spoilers here.
