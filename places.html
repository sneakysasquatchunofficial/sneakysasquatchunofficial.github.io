Places

Main areas only for now. Under the hood the game has way more scenes than this, since every building floor, cave and underwater bit is its own scene, but these are the ones you'd actually call places.

Campground

Where you start. Campsites, the RV park, a lake, a river, the parking lot. Your cave is up here too.

Town

Shops, people, the town hall. Gets busy.

Golf Course

You can play, or caddy. There's a driving range and a long drive area.

Racetrack

Car racing, with a dirt track close by.

Seaport and Marina

The seaport is the big harbor that comes in later in the story. Boats are at the marina.

Farm

Farm, plus a small farm town next to it.

Island

Out across the water. I'll leave that one for you to find.
